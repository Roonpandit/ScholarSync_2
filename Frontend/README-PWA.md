# ScholarSync Progressive Web App (PWA) Installation Guide

ScholarSync is now available as a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your device for a more app-like experience.

## Installation Instructions

### On Mobile Devices (iOS & Android)

1. **Open in Chrome/Safari**
   - Open Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) on your device
   - Navigate to the ScholarSync website

2. **Install the App**
   - **Android/Chrome:**
     1. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner
     2. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
     3. Confirm by tapping "Add" or "Install"
   - **iOS/Safari:**
     1. Tap the share button (box with up arrow)
     2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
     3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

3. **Access the App**
   - The app will now be available on your home screen
   - Tap the icon to open it in full-screen mode

### On Desktop (Windows/macOS/Linux)

1. **Using Chrome/Edge**
   - Open the website in Chrome or Edge
   - Look for the install icon (⎙) in the address bar
   - Click "Install" to add to your desktop

2. **Using Other Browsers**
   - Check the browser menu for "Install" or "Add to Home Screen"
   - Follow the prompts to complete installation

## Offline Access

Once installed, ScholarSync can work offline for most features:
- View previously loaded data
- Navigate between pages
- Some functionality may be limited without an internet connection

## Updates

The app will automatically update in the background. You'll be notified when a new version is available.

## Troubleshooting

If you're having issues:
1. Make sure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox)
2. Clear your browser cache if the app doesn't update
3. Ensure you have a stable internet connection during installation

## Benefits of Installing as PWA

- Faster loading times
- Works offline
- Uses less data
- App-like experience
- No app store downloads required
- Always up-to-date
